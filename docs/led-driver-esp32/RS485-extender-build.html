RS-485 Differential Extension — WS2801 Inter-Strand Bridge

Bridges the ~24 ft gap between LED strand 1 (end) and strand 2 (start) of one 800-pixel WS2801 chain, carrying the 5 V CKO/SDO clock and data differentially over one Cat6 run. Simplex, one direction, no direction switching.  ·  Lightbringer project · 2026-08

Enable mnemonic — DRIVER end (strand 1): pins 2+3 tied HIGH (5 V). RECEIVER end (strand 2): pins 2+3 tied LOW (GND).

Schematic

Four chips total: 2 drivers at the strand-1 end, 2 receivers at the strand-2 start — one each for CLOCK and DATA. ST485BN and MAX485CPA are interchangeable here (identical pinout, 5 V, 2.5 Mbps); use four of the same part if stock allows, otherwise keep each signal path homogeneous (both CLOCK chips one type, both DATA chips the other). Each end is powered from the local 5 V injection already present at its strand — no logic power crosses the gap.

Cat6 pair assignment

OrangeDATA A/B

Never run LED power over the Cat6. The gap carries the clock pair, data pair, and ground bond — nothing else. No series resistors on the short chip↔WS2801 hops (they are inches, not transmission lines); the only termination in this stage is the 120 Ω at each receiver.

Bring-up sequence

  1. Wire and power both ends from their local 5 V. With the controller idle, a scope across A–B at the receiver shows a clean differential idle (the driver is always on — the line is never floating).
  2. Run at the chain's standard clock — currently 1.5 MHz (hw.led.ins[0].freq: 1500, validated on the installed array). Push full white plus a fast effect; watch strand-2 pixels for sparkle or wrong colours.
  3. If strand 2 shows corruption, drop to 1 MHz (freq: 1000) — a runtime config change, no reflash. 800 px at 1 MHz still refreshes ~52 Hz, above WLED's 42 fps target: no visible cost.
  4. Pass criterion: strand 1 and strand 2 pixel-identical side by side under motion.
